# Tainacan Hooks

In adittion to the [WordPress core hooks](https://developer.wordpress.org/plugins/hooks/), Tainacan has its own hooks, which offer developers opportunities to **expand or alterate the behaviour os certain sections of code**. Hooks are divided into two types: [Actions](actions.md) and [Filters](filters.md). Check their documentation bellow:

- [Tainacan Actions](actions.md)
- [Tainacan Filters](filters.md)
